# M4 — Adaptive Guidance Runtime

**Status: OPEN — M4-A COMPLETE; M4-B COMPLETE / locally validated; M4-C is next**

[ADR 0017](../decisions/0017-product-runtime-v1-adaptive-guidance.md) is the
product-direction authority for this milestone. M4 must preserve the existing
ports/adapters architecture and must not rewrite M3's historical evaluation
evidence.

## Scope

M4 turns the M3 Judge foundation into an AI-required, adaptive two-turn daily
experience. It derives a product learner state from semantic Judge/node and known
misconception signals, deterministically chooses a guidance action, and selects
the next response from approved content rather than allowing unrestricted model
generation.

Normal flow is fixed:

1. provider readiness gate;
2. show today's problem;
3. Turn 1 reasoning;
4. semantic-state analysis;
5. adaptive guidance;
6. Turn 2 reasoning;
7. semantic-state analysis;
8. Reveal; and
9. achievement label and personalized comparison.

There are exactly two reasoning turns and no third turn.

## Learner states and guidance actions

M4 derives:

- `OFF_TRACK`
- `MISCONCEPTION`
- `ON_TRACK`
- `NEAR_COMPLETE`
- `COMPLETE_LIKELY`

The default deterministic action mapping is:

| Learner state | Guidance action |
| --- | --- |
| `OFF_TRACK` | `REDIRECT` |
| `MISCONCEPTION` | `CORRECT` |
| `ON_TRACK` | `TARGET` |
| `NEAR_COMPLETE` | `BRIDGE` |
| `COMPLETE_LIKELY` | `CONSOLIDATE` |

Content will supply an approved Hint Ladder or guidance pool. M4 may introduce
that content shape without creating Conway v6 merely as preparation in advance.
`COMPLETE_LIKELY` receives consolidation for Turn 2 and must not reveal correctness
or canonical theory before Reveal.

## Provider readiness and recovery

AI feedback is required for official play. Provider unavailability must prevent a
new official session from starting rather than silently falling back to a static
quiz. Readiness should be shared or cached server-side where practical; it should
not require a dedicated canary request for every individual player.

If the provider fails after a session starts, M4 preserves the session, pauses
progression, does not consume or invalidate the daily play, does not force Reveal,
and permits resumption at the same point after provider capability returns.

## Achievement and data boundary

No user-facing scalar intellectual score is required. Runtime v1 uses:

- `독립 재발견`
- `힌트 후 도달`
- `핵심 일부 포착`
- `Reveal에서 연결`

The implementation should retain structured semantic/progression facts useful to
future analytics, but M4 does not implement the M8 analytics system.

## Preserved verifier seam

The Final Synthesis verifier remains a **PRESERVED RESEARCH ASSET — NOT A RUNTIME
V1 RELEASE BLOCKER**. M4 must not delete or rewrite it. A future runtime may
substitute or augment the lightweight semantic sensor through the existing
port/adapter boundary without redesigning the game.

## Acceptance criteria

M4 is complete only when all of the following are verified:

1. Official play is exactly two reasoning turns.
2. Turn 1 Judge output resolves to a learner state.
3. Learner state selects a deterministic guidance action.
4. Guidance is selected from approved content, not unrestricted model generation.
5. Different missing concepts can result in different guidance.
6. Known misconceptions can trigger corrective guidance.
7. Near-complete reasoning receives a smaller bridge than off-track reasoning.
8. `COMPLETE_LIKELY` does not reveal correctness before Reveal.
9. Turn 2 receives semantic analysis but no third reasoning turn.
10. AI unavailability prevents new official play through a readiness mechanism.
11. Mid-session provider failure preserves and pauses the session for later resume.
12. No user-facing scalar score is required.
13. Existing Final Synthesis research remains intact and replaceable.
14. Normal successful gameplay is designed around one or two semantic provider
    evaluations per daily play, excluding shared readiness canaries.

The implemented sub-gates below record local deterministic/browser verification.
Readiness and pause/resume criteria remain open for M4-C; M4 is not yet complete.

## M4-B — Two-Turn Runtime Wiring — COMPLETE

Adaptive content branches by `adaptive_guidance` capability; nonadaptive content
retains its existing Lock/Final Synthesis behavior. After validated Judge output
and deterministic merge, Turn 1 selects approved guidance and remains THINKING
with turnCount 1, including COMPLETE_LIKELY/CONSOLIDATE. Turn 2 always calls Judge
again, resolves merged final learner state, persists generic terminal feedback,
and becomes REVEAL_READY with turnCount 2. A third answer is rejected before Judge.
No adaptive path enters SYNTHESIZING or LOCKABLE, or confirms correctness.

Terminal feedback maps COMPLETE_LIKELY to comparing the player's organized
thinking, NEAR_COMPLETE to comparing the final relationship, ON_TRACK to comparing
connections between clues, MISCONCEPTION to comparing diverging relationships,
and OFF_TRACK to considering a newly introduced perspective. It contains no
hidden identity, correctness verdict, evidence literals, or numerical score.

Guidance uses existing storage keys:
`adaptive-v1:1:<learner-state>:<action>:<target-or-empty>` and
`adaptive-v1:2:<learner-state>`. Approved text is reconstructed on read, and learner
state remains derivable from discoveries. No DB migration was needed.

Public/API projection exposes approved text and minimal adaptive capabilities.
The existing Play surface keeps Turn 2 input after Turn 1, removes input after
Turn 2, shows terminal feedback even after reload, and offers an explicit Reveal
button. Reveal continues through the existing path without inventing verified
evidence or a self-discovery claim. Final Synthesis research remains intact and
Conway v5 is unchanged.

Conway v6 remains **UNSCHEDULED / LIVE DISABLED** until M4-C closes. Runtime v1
requires semantic AI availability. **No AI-free degraded mode is permitted.**
Provider failures retain existing recoverable error behavior in this gate.
